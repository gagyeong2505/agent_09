## Title

2027 Suneung D-day checklist: what to bring, banned items, timetable and score release date

## Body

[Image 01: 01_thumbnail_omr_answer_sheet.jpg — representative image]

Note: all dates and figures in this post are as of October 31, 2026.

The 2027 Suneung is less than three weeks away. The exam is on Thursday, November 19, 2026.
In the last stretch, avoiding mistakes on the day matters as much as studying.

This year has new rules: a provisional-scoring sheet on the back of the admission ticket, personal computer pens allowed, and smart glasses banned.
Here is everything from what to bring to the timetable and score release date, for students and parents.

### 1. Key dates for the 2027 Suneung (as of October 31, 2026)

| Item | Date |
|---|---|
| Orientation and admission ticket handout | Wed, Nov 18, 2026 |
| Exam day | Thu, Nov 19, 2026, 08:40–17:45 |
| Objections to questions/answers | Nov 19 – Nov 23, 2026 |
| Answers finalized | Dec 1, 2026 |
| Scores released | Fri, Dec 11, 2026 |

- Orientation time and place differ by school and exam district; check with your school or education office.
- Current students get scores at school; graduates and GED takers get them where they applied or online from 09:00.
- Weather for each test site is on the KMA website from November 13.
- KICE says the schedule may change depending on circumstances.

### 2. Exam-day timetable (as of October 31, 2026)

[Image 03: 03_classroom_students.jpg]

All students must be in the exam room by 08:10. From period 2, be seated 10 minutes before each period.

| Period | Subject | Time | Questions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Korean | 08:40–10:00 (80 min) | 45 |
| 2 | Math | 10:30–12:10 (100 min) | 30 |
| Lunch | - | 12:10–13:00 | - |
| 3 | English | 13:10–14:20 (70 min) | 45 (listening 13:10–13:35) |
| 4 | Korean History | 14:50–15:20 (30 min) | 20 |
| 4 | Inquiry, 1st elective | 15:35–16:05 (30 min) | 20 |
| 4 | Inquiry, 2nd elective | 16:07–16:37 (30 min) | 20 |
| 5 | 2nd Foreign Language/Classical Chinese | 17:05–17:45 (40 min) | 30 |

KICE says question counts and times are the same as last year.

### 3. Three changes this year

[Image 04: 04_desk_calculator_pen.jpg]

1. Provisional-scoring sheet printed on the back of the admission ticket
   - Usable only during exam time.
   - Writing on it after the end bell is cheating and voids the exam.
2. Personal computer pens allowed
   - Black computer pens are still handed out at the test site.
   - A personal pen that meets the requirements may be used, but not one with disallowed features such as an attached red pen.
   - Note: check the detailed pen requirements in the KICE notice for test takers (수험생 유의사항) or with your school or education office.
3. Smart glasses explicitly banned
   - Wearing smart glasses voids this year's exam and suspends eligibility for the 2028 exam for one year.
   - If a proctor asks, take off your glasses and place them on the desk for inspection.

### 4. What to bring (checklist)

[Image 02: 02_pencil_writing.jpg]

- [ ] Admission ticket: handed out at the Nov 18 orientation. Check your electives are printed correctly.
- [ ] Physical ID only: resident card, driver's license, valid passport, youth ID card, or student ID with name, birth date, photo and principal's seal. Mobile IDs are not accepted.
- [ ] Analog watch: hour and minute hands only, no communication/payment functions, no electronic display
- [ ] White correction tape (also available in the exam room)
- [ ] Black 0.5 mm pencil leads, black pencil, eraser
- [ ] Packed lunch and water (lunch 12:10–13:00)
- [ ] Warm outer layer
- [ ] If needed: mask, hearing aid, magnifier, earplugs, cushion (allowed after proctor check)

Notes:
- Mechanical pencils are handed out in the exam room before period 1; personal ones are confiscated.
- Study materials must be stored in a designated place before the exam starts; having them during the exam is cheating.
- Note: if you lose your admission ticket, get it reissued before exam day. For the exam-day reissue procedure, check the KICE notice for test takers or your school or education office.

### 5. Banned items and penalties

Do not bring:
- Phones, smartwatches, tablets, wireless earphones (communication/payment/Bluetooth)
- Smart glasses (newly listed for 2027)
- Electronic dictionaries, digital watches, tumblers with screens (electronic displays)
- Power banks, e-cigarettes (rechargeable items)

If you brought any, hand them to the proctor before period 1. Keeping them voids the exam. They are returned after your last subject.

| Violation | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Looking at others' answers, using unauthorized materials/wireless devices, proxy test-taking, etc. | Exam void + 1-year suspension for next year |
| Wearing smart glasses during the exam | 2027 exam void + 1-year suspension for 2028 |
| Keeping banned items without handing them in before period 1 | Exam void |
| Writing/changing answers after the end bell (including the scoring sheet) | Exam void |
| Breaking period-4 procedures | Exam void |

From 2022 to 2026 there were 1,229 cheating cases; the most common was writing answers or the scoring sheet after the end bell (479 cases, 39.0%). Put your pen down as soon as the bell rings.

### 6. Period 4 (Korean History and Inquiry) rules

- Korean History is mandatory; skipping it voids the whole exam and no scores are issued.
- Keep only the current subject's booklet on the desk; put the 2nd-elective booklet in the personal envelope.
- Writing or changing 1st-elective answers during the 2nd-elective time is cheating.
- Students not taking Inquiry move to the waiting room during the 15:20–15:35 booklet change.
- Single-subject Inquiry takers have been told to wait during the 1st-elective time and test in the 2nd-elective time.
- Note: check the single-subject Inquiry rule in the KICE notice for test takers or with your school or education office.

### 7. Exam-day support

Per the Ministry of Education (as of October 31, 2026):
- Public offices and companies are asked to start work at 10:00 or later.
- Extra Seoul-area subway trains run 06:00–08:10.
- Vehicles are blocked from 200 m in front of test sites; get dropped off early and walk.
- During English listening (13:10–13:35), aircraft takeoffs/landings are adjusted, and buses/trains near test sites slow down and do not honk.
- The exam runs at 1,312 test sites in 85 districts nationwide.

### 8. Why the 2027 Suneung is special

- 551,864 applicants (2,310 fewer than last year), as of October 31, 2026.
- 173,706 graduates (31.5%), the most since the 2004 exam.
- This is the last exam under the current "common + elective" system.
- From the 2028 exam (Nov 18, 2027), Korean, Math and Inquiry have no electives.

### Closing: final check the night before

- Pack admission ticket, physical ID, analog watch, correction tape, lunch and water, outer layer
- Leave early enough to arrive well before 08:10 (allow for the 200 m vehicle block)
- Hand in phone, smartwatch, earphones, smart glasses, power bank before period 1
- Use the scoring sheet only during exam time; put the pen down at the end bell

Good luck to every test taker!

# LEGODT (레고트) — Tumbler Brand Research for a Brand Introduction Web Page

- Date: 2026-11-01
- Scope: Brand overview, company, product lineup and specs, design/color concept, prices, sales channels, reviews, target users, brand story, visual identity cues, and a proposed web page structure. Research is based on public web pages (official store legodt.kr, retailer pages, Korean and Japanese press). Prices are snapshots taken on 2026-11-01 and change often. Brand product images were not downloaded (copyright). Items that could not be confirmed are marked **[Unverified]** or **[Not found]**.

## Summary

- **Exact name:** The brand is written **LEGODT** in Latin letters and **레고트** in Korean (official store: legodt.kr; Japan: legodt.jp). It is a Korean lifestyle brand, not related to LEGO. Spellings such as "레고디티" were not found in use [1][2].
- **Company:** LEGODT started as a "contemporary lifestyle brand" spun out of the ceramic/tableware brand **Odense (오덴세)** [2]. Both are run by **Brandworks Korea Co., Ltd. (주식회사 브랜드웍스코리아)**, a brand subsidiary of **CJ ENM** that launched on 2023-01-02 [3][4][5]. A 2026 article calls it "CJ OnStyle's lifestyle brand" [6].
- **Core product:** The **Loop Tumbler** line. It is 304 stainless steel with double-wall vacuum insulation and a powder-coated exterior. A wide loop handle is built into the lid, and the silicone sleeves can be swapped. Sizes run from 200 ml to 1000 ml (Mini, Standard, Multi, Handy, Two-way, Module, plus a Tritan plastic version). Official store prices are about KRW 29,000–42,000 [1][5][7].
- **Brand concept:** Playful, colorful, customizable. The brand built the **"TUMGGU (텀꾸, tumbler decorating)"** trend with keyrings, stickers and straps, and ran pop-ups in Seoul, Japan and Singapore. In 2026 it moved to a **modular tumbler with 28 combinable parts**. June 2026 sales were reported about 45% higher than a year earlier [6][8][9][10][11].
- **Target:** Gen Z / MZ consumers who use everyday items to express their taste. Collaborations include Pokémon (29CM, July 2026), Mangom (Olive Young) and Fluffy (pop-up), and there is reported demand in Japan [6][12][13][14].

## 1. Name, origin and company

- **Name and spelling:** The official store header reads "LEGODT 레고트 공식 스토어" [1]. Retailers (29CM, Musinsa, Danawa) and the Japanese press use "LEGODT" / "레고트" [2][7][15][16]. No source uses "레고디티" or other variants. Search results for "lego tumbler" mostly return LEGO's own products, which are unrelated, so keep this possible confusion in mind for SEO and naming.
- **Country:** South Korea. The Japanese press calls it a "Korea-born new-sensation tumbler brand" [16]. Products are **made in China** according to the official product notices [5][7].
- **Company:**
  - The official store footer and product notices list **Brandworks Korea Co., Ltd. (주식회사 브랜드웍스코리아)** as the operator and importer/seller. CEO: Park Seong-bae. Customer service: 1833-2623 [1][5].
  - Brandworks Korea is a **CJ ENM** brand-business subsidiary. It was formed by renaming ArtWorks Korea, took over brands such as Odense, Brooks Brothers and Danielle Cremieux, and launched on 2023-01-02 [3][4].
  - 29CM describes LEGODT as "a contemporary lifestyle brand newly launched by the ceramic specialist brand Odense" [2]. A Money Today article (2026-08-07) calls it "CJ OnStyle's lifestyle brand LEGODT" [6]. CJ OnStyle is CJ ENM's commerce division.
- **Parent brand Odense:** It first launched through CJ home shopping in **2013** and is known for Nordic-style, affordable tableware [17].
- **Founding / launch date:** **[Not found]** No official launch date was found. Earliest clues: Danawa lists "Odense LEGODT Loop Tumbler 400ml" as registered in **March 2023** [15], and the Odense online store listed LEGODT loop tumbler models with release dates around September 2023 to March 2024 (seen in search snippets only; the pages now return 404) **[Unverified]**.
- **Name origin:** **[Unverified]** A CJ newsroom interview says the LEGODT product idea was inspired by "a scene of Nordic children playing with pieces of wooden log" [17]. No source explains the word "LEGODT" itself. (Danish *leg* means "play", but no source links the two, so do not state this as fact.)

- **TUMGGU (텀꾸):** The brand icon/keyword combines "tumbler" and the Korean verb *kkumida* (to decorate) [16]. Reporting links the trend to the US Stanley-cup customization craze among Gen Z / Gen Alpha [18].

## 3. Product lineup

### 3.1 Loop Tumbler line (stainless)

Common features across the official product pages [5][7]:
- **Body:** 304 stainless steel, powder-coated exterior; **double-wall vacuum insulation**. The interior is polished. The exterior is not polished, and the maker recommends polishing it before use.
- **Loop handle:** A wide ring-shaped handle built into the lid. Grip diameter is at least 50 mm on Standard and at least 80 mm on Multi 1000 ml, which can rotate 180°.
- **Silicone sleeve:** Heat-resistant and interchangeable between colors ("mix & match").
- **Wide mouth:** 77 mm (Standard 400 ml) and 90 mm (Multi 1000 ml), so ice, yogurt or ice cream fit.
- **Care:** Hand wash only (not dishwasher-safe). No boiling, microwave or freezer. **Not fully leak-proof**: carry upright.
- Some Odense-sold Two-way models have a **ceramic-coated interior** (304 stainless + ceramic coating), which reviewers say reduces the metallic smell [19].

## 4. Design and color concept

- **Colors:** Many cheerful pastels plus a few neutrals. Colors seen on the official store include light gray, mint, mint green, mint blue, berry pink, coral, lavender, ivory, vanilla cream, yellow, lemon soda, lime, orange/carrot, sweet pumpkin, tangerine yellow, light blue, blue, navy, green, red, camel, brown, dark gray, and **gradient** variants (lavender, blue, pink) [1][15][19].
- **Form:** Rounded, simple cylinder with a **large ring ("loop") handle** on the lid. This loop is the brand's signature shape and the source of the product name [5][7].
- **Customization as design:** Swappable sleeves, a modular body/lid/handle (28 parts) and decorating accessories [6][10].
- **Japanese press description:** "colorful, pop design, cute silhouette, abundant customization"; bright colors plus neutral shades suited to office, gym and park [16].

## 7. Customer reviews and strengths

- **Ratings:** Musinsa, Loop Tumbler Standard 400 ml: **4.8 / 5 from 199 reviews** (2026-11-01) [23]. Individual review text could not be extracted.
- **Independent spec review (Nosearch, Two-way 450 ml)** [19]:
  - Pros: does not leak in carrying, ceramic interior reduces metallic smell, handle on the lid is easy to carry.
  - Cons: narrow inlet (brush needed), lid gasket not removable (harder to clean), screw lid slow to open, clip mouthpiece can touch the nose. Overall score 76.4 (13th of 16); weakest on price score.
- **Strengths drawn from the sources:** many colors, a recognizable loop handle, customization (sleeves, modules, accessories), wide mouth for ice, large sizes with a straw (1000 ml), and strong pop-up/collab marketing.
- **Weak points to be honest about:** not fully leak-proof (official notice) [5], hand wash only, made in China.

## 8. Target users

- Gen Z / MZ consumers who enjoy decorating and self-expression (TUMGGU) [6][18]. Example: a 24-year-old university student quoted as finding it "more fun to build a tumbler from my chosen colors and parts than to buy a finished one" [6].
- Daily commuters, office workers, gym users, picnic and travel users (store collections "Daily Sip", "Move Light", "Picnic Season"; Japanese release mentions office/gym/park) [1][16].
- Character and collab fans (Pokémon, Mangom) and Japanese/Asian tourists and shoppers [12][16][17].

## 10. Visual identity notes for the web designer

- **Logo:** Wordmark "LEGODT" in Latin capitals, shown next to "레고트" on the official store [1]. A short "odt" mark appears in product names (e.g. "odt eco bag") [1]. **[Unverified]** The exact logo typeface and logo color could not be confirmed from text-only fetches. Check the live site (legodt.kr) and Instagram (@legodt.official) before designing, and get official logo files from the brand.
- **Mood:** Playful, bright, friendly, "fun"; pop and cute rather than premium/minimal-luxury [2][16].
- **Photography style (observed on official store):** Soft pastel-leaning product shots on plain backgrounds; category tiles; a "Make Your Own" customization block; a "Shop the Video" section [1]. Pop-up visuals use game/arcade themes (LV.1–LV.3 zones) [9].
- **Copy style:** Short, friendly English headlines ("Fresh Start", "Make Your Own", "Loved By You") mixed with Korean body text [1].
- **Suggested palette for a page (designer's choice, inferred from product colors, not an official brand palette):** off-white/ivory base; accent pastels mint, lavender, coral/berry pink, lemon yellow, light blue; dark navy or charcoal for text.

## 11. Proposed web page structure and tone

**Tone:** Bright, playful, "play-your-way" voice. Use short English hooks with Korean explanations. Make it interactive (color pickers, mix-and-match). State all specs carefully and add "based on the maker's own test" next to insulation numbers. Avoid suggesting any link to LEGO.

Suggested sections (one-page scroll):

1. **Hero:** "LEGODT — Play My Way" (use an existing brand phrase) with a loop tumbler visual and colorful background shapes; CTA "Make Your Own".
2. **Brand Story:** Born from Odense's Nordic ceramic roots (Nordic children playing with wooden logs); a colorful lifestyle brand for self-expression (Brandworks Korea / CJ ENM).
3. **Signature: the Loop:** loop handle, wide mouth, double-wall vacuum 304 stainless, swappable silicone sleeve (icon cards).
4. **Lineup:** cards for Mini 200, Standard 400, Module 414, Multi 600/1000, Handy 700/1000, Tritan 532, with capacity, key feature and price range.
5. **Make Your Own / TUMGGU:** a simple interactive color/part mixer (body + lid + sleeve colors), mention 28 modular parts and accessories (keyrings, stickers, straps).
6. **Color Palette:** swatch grid of the official color names (mint, lavender, coral, lemon soda, gradient pink, …).
7. **Collabs & Pop-ups:** timeline (Musinsa Seongsu 2024 → Olive Young N Seongsu 2025 → Japan/Singapore 2025 → COEX Module 2026 → Pokémon 2026).
8. **Loved By You:** Musinsa 4.8/5 (199 reviews) plus short, genuine review points (do not invent quotes; use placeholders until real reviews are approved).
9. **Where to Buy:** legodt.kr, 29CM, Musinsa, Olive Young, legodt.jp; Instagram links.
10. **Care Guide / FAQ:** hand wash, no microwave/freezer, not fully leak-proof, polish exterior before first use.
11. **Footer:** brand/company info (Brandworks Korea), disclaimer that this is an unofficial introduction page if it is not commissioned by the brand.

## Outlook / implications

- LEGODT is moving from "decorate a finished tumbler" (2024–2025) to "assemble your own tumbler" (2026 modular), and is widening into bags, travel goods and lunchboxes. A web page should put **customization** and **color** at the center.
- Growth depends on pop-ups, IP collaborations and the Japan/Asia expansion. A timeline or "news" section fits the brand well.
- Risks: The customization trend depends on fads. The tumbler market is crowded (Stanley, Hydro Flask, local brands). Some products are not leak-proof or dishwasher-safe, so specs should be presented accurately.

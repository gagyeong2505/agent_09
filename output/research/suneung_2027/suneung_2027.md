# 2027 Korean CSAT (2027학년도 수능, 19 Nov 2026): D-day Checklist for Test-takers and Parents

- Date: 2026-10-31
- Scope: Key dates, exam-day timetable, items to bring, banned items and penalties, Period 4 (Korean History / Inquiry) rules, transport and practical support, the status of this exam as the last "elective-format" CSAT, and test-taker discounts. Official sources first: KICE (한국교육과정평가원, suneung.re.kr), Ministry of Education (교육부, via korea.kr) and provincial education offices (as reported by the press). Where only press or blog summaries were available, this is flagged. The KICE "수험생 유의사항" document (posted 2026-10-15) itself could not be opened directly (attachment); its rules are taken from press reports and secondary summaries that cite it, and from the 2026 version where the 2027 wording could not be seen. Intended use: source note for a Korean Naver Blog post.

## Summary

- The exam is on **Thursday 19 November 2026**. Arrive in the exam room by **08:10**. Scores come out on **Friday 11 December 2026**. KICE takes objections to questions and answers from **19 to 23 November** and will publish the final answers on **1 December** [1][2].
- **New this year:** (1) An answer-tally sheet (가채점표) is printed on the back of the admission ticket (수험표). You may fill it in only during exam time. Writing on it after the end bell counts as cheating. (2) You may use your own black computer-marking pen (컴퓨터용 사인펜) if it meets the requirements, and the exam site still hands one out. (3) **Smart glasses are now explicitly banned.** Wearing them voids the exam and also suspends eligibility for next year's CSAT [3][4][6].
- Phones, smartwatches, earphones, tablets, e-dictionaries, anything with an electronic display, and rechargeable items (power banks, e-cigarettes) are banned. If you brought one, hand it in before Period 1 starts. Failing to do so voids the exam [5][6][7].
- Korean History (한국사) is mandatory. Skipping it voids the whole CSAT. In Period 4, keep only the paper for the subject currently being tested on the desk. Answering or fixing first-choice answers during the second-choice slot counts as cheating [3][7][8].
- 551,864 people applied, and the share of graduate re-takers is the highest since 2004. This is the **last CSAT in the current "common + elective" format**. From the 2028 CSAT (Nov 2027), Korean, Math and Inquiry have no electives [9][10][11].

Sources: KB Think / Daehak Naeil summary citing KICE (2026-10-14) [5]; braindetox summary citing the KICE detailed plan and guidance (2026-10-17) [7]. The MOE confirms that the exam runs 08:40 to 17:45 and that the English listening section runs 13:10 to 13:35 [13]. KICE says the number of questions and the exam times are the same as last year [12][15].

**Period 4 rules**
- Korean History is mandatory for everyone. If you do not take it, your whole CSAT is invalid and you receive no scores [3][5].
- Candidates who did not choose Inquiry go to a waiting room during the 15:20 to 15:35 paper change [7].
- You may skip the whole Inquiry section, but you cannot skip just one of your chosen subjects. A candidate with two subjects may leave after the first subject. However, you cannot come in only for the second subject [5].
- Candidates taking **only one** Inquiry subject wait during the first-choice slot and sit their subject in the second-choice slot. This comes from a secondary summary of the 2026 KICE guide and is expected to be unchanged, but it was not verified in the 2027 original [8].
- **Handling the extra paper:** keep **only the current subject's paper** on the desk. The second-choice paper stays in the personal paper-storage envelope. Any paper that is neither your first nor your second choice is taken away and cannot be swapped [8][16].
- Looking at another subject's paper, having two papers out at once, or writing or fixing first-choice answers during the second-choice slot counts as **cheating**. The exam is void. In the 2025 CSAT, 48 candidates lost their exam for marking first-choice answers during the second-choice slot [8][16].

## 4. Banned items and penalties

**Banned from the exam site** [5][6][7][8]:
- Anything with a communication or payment function, Bluetooth included: phones, smartwatches, tablets, wireless earphones
- **Smart glasses (newly listed for 2027)**
- Anything with an electronic display (LCD/LED): e-dictionaries, digital watches, display tumblers
- Rechargeable items: power banks, e-cigarettes

**If you brought one:** hand it to the proctor **before Period 1 starts**. You get it back after your last subject [8].

**Penalties** (based on the CSAT management rules, MOE directive, Article 19) [6][8]:

| Violation | Result |
|---|---|
| Category 1 to 5 cheating (looking at others' answers, signalling, using banned materials or wireless devices, proxy test-taking, coercion) | That year's exam void, plus **next year's CSAT eligibility suspended for 1 year** |
| **Wearing smart glasses during the exam**, or refusing a proctor's check (treated as Category 3) | 2027 exam void, plus 2028 CSAT eligibility suspended for 1 year |
| Having a banned item without handing it in before Period 1 (Category 9) | Exam void |
| Writing or fixing answers after the end bell, **including filling in the 가채점표 after the bell** | Exam void |
| Breaking the Period 4 subject-order rules | Exam void |
| Refusing an ID check or bag check, or ignoring the proctor's instructions | Exam void |

- If a proctor asks, take off your glasses and put them on the desk for checking [6].
- From 2022 to 2026 there were 1,229 CSAT cheating cases. The most common, at 479 cases (39.0%), was writing answers or 가채점표 entries after the end bell [6].
- Teacher unions (전교조 and 한국교총) said on 2026-10-20 that putting smart-glasses screening on individual proctors places too much burden on them. Expect stricter glasses checks on the day [17].

## 5. Practical tips from official guidance

**Government support on exam day** (MOE, reported to the Cabinet on 2026-10-20) [13][18]:
- Government offices and companies are asked to start work **at 10:00 or later**.
- More metropolitan-area subway trains run during the **06:00 to 08:10** commute window. Police and local governments put emergency transport vehicles on test-takers' routes.
- **Vehicles cannot enter within 200 m of an exam site.** If you are being driven, get out early and walk the last 200 m.
- During the **English listening section (13:10 to 13:35)**, aircraft take-off and landing times are adjusted and military firing drills stop. Buses and trains near sites slow down and do not sound their horns.
- Weather for each exam site is on the **KMA website from 13 November**. Local governments prepare transport for island and remote areas, plus snow-clearing plans.
- 1,312 exam sites in 85 districts nationwide. Exam-day staff include police at each venue [13].

**About "call 112/119 if you are running late":** in past years police transported late candidates in patrol cars or on motorcycles after a 112 call (e.g. the 2012 press report). However, **the 2027 MOE plan does not mention a 112 or 119 transport service**. Treat it as an emergency fallback only, not as an official 2027 service [18][19]. Not confirmed for 2027.

**Practical checklist (drawn from the official items above):**
- At orientation on 11-18: get your 수험표, check that your chosen subjects are printed correctly, and visit the venue to check the route and travel time. Entry rules for the venue building on that day are set locally, so check your local notice.
- Pack the night before: 수험표, physical photo ID, analogue watch, correction tape, lunch box and water, a warm layer.
- Leave home early enough to be in the room well before 08:10. Remember the 200 m vehicle cut-off.
- Before Period 1: hand in your phone, smartwatch, earphones, smart glasses and power bank.
- During each period, use the 가채점표 only during exam time and stop the moment the end bell rings.

## 6. Last "elective-format" CSAT: confirmed

- The 2027 CSAT keeps the "common + elective" structure introduced in the 2022 CSAT [3][10].
  - Korean: common section plus one of 화법과 작문 or 언어와 매체.
  - Math: common section plus one of 확률과 통계, 미적분 or 기하.
  - Inquiry: up to 2 of 17 social or science subjects.
  - English, Korean History and 2nd foreign language/Classical Chinese are graded on an absolute scale.
  - EBS linkage is indirect, at about 50% [3].
- From the **2028 CSAT (exam on 18 Nov 2027)**, Korean and Math have no electives and everyone sits the same questions. Inquiry becomes 통합사회 and 통합과학 with no choice [10][11]. Press reports call the 2027 exam the "last 선택형 수능" or "last year of the current CSAT" [9][10].
- This explains the 2027 application data [9]:
  - **Applicants:** 551,864 in total (−2,310), of whom 355,391 are at school and 173,706 are graduates (31.5%, the highest since 2004).
  - **Social-studies shift (사탐런):** 69.6% of Inquiry takers chose only social studies.
  - **Probability-and-statistics shift (확통런):** 64.9% of math takers chose 확률과 통계.

## 7. Test-taker discounts

- **As of 2026-10-31, no 2027-CSAT (Nov 2026) discount campaigns had been published.** Search results only returned the previous year's (2026-CSAT, Nov 2025) offers [20][21].
- Last year's pattern is a guide, not a confirmation:
  - Showing your 수험표 got discounts at theme parks (Everland, Lotte World), cinemas, aquariums, airlines (e.g. Jeju Air for test-takers and families) and fashion retailers.
  - Offers usually ran from the day after the exam until about February [20][21].
- Most campaigns are announced in the week of the exam. Keep your 수험표, because it serves as proof.

## Outlook / implications

- **Biggest risks this year for blog readers:**
  - Smart glasses and other electronics: simply having one voids the exam, and wearing smart glasses also costs next year.
  - The new 가채점표: writing on it after the bell voids the exam, and this is already the most common violation.
  - The Period 4 paper order.
- **Stakes for re-takers:** graduate numbers are at a 23-year high. Because 2028 moves to an integrated format, a failed 2027 attempt means re-preparing under a different exam. The 1-year suspension for smart-glasses cheating is therefore especially costly.
- **Check before publishing:**
  - Orientation times are set locally. Tell readers to confirm with their school or education office.
  - Discount offers should be added after the exam week, once companies announce them.
  - Sentences on the one-subject Inquiry rule, the lost-수험표 procedure and 112 transport should carry "check the KICE/local notice" wording.

## Unconfirmed / gaps

- The KICE 2027 수험생 유의사항 original (posted 2026-10-15) was not opened directly. Its rules come from press and secondary summaries citing it, and from the 2026 guide.
- **Orientation on 11-18:** only one regional education office (Jeonnam-Gwangju) confirmed this via the press. Other regions are likely the same but were not verified.
- **Own computer-marking pen:** the full list of requirements was not found.
- **Lost-수험표 procedure on exam day** (report by 08:00 with a photo): taken from a secondary source only.
- **112/119 transport:** no 2027 official mention.
- **Discounts:** none announced yet.
- **Listening and aircraft timing:** Nocut News gives 13:05 to 13:40, which is probably the aircraft-control window. The MOE gives 13:10 to 13:35 as the listening time.

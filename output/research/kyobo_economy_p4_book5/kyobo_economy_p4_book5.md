## Summary

The author is a tax accountant and well-known stock investor. He argues that becoming
wealthy is a matter of math, not luck. Wealth grows through two factors: income growth
rate (**G**) and investment return rate (**R**). The book lays out a three-step roadmap:

1. Set a target asset level, then grow assets step by step by managing income and returns.
2. Maximize compound growth with practical trading rules.
3. After retirement, use the **4% rule** to keep a lifelong cash flow.

The publisher's copy says the author was invited to a presidential meeting on capital
market stability. It also calls him a writer whose books reached #1 at major bookstores.
These are marketing claims and were not verified.

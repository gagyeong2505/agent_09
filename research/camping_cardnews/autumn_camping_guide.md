# Card News Plan: "Now Is Prime Camping Season" — Enjoying Autumn Camping 200%

- **Topic**: Camping (Plan #5 of 5 candidates)
- **Date**: 2026-10-25
- **Channel**: Instagram carousel, 4:5 ratio (1080 × 1350 px)
- **Target**: Beginner to intermediate campers in their 20s–30s (couples, friend groups) planning a weekend trip
- **Total cards**: 8 (Card 1 = thumbnail)
- **Language note**: Card copy is written in Korean (as it will appear on the cards); everything else is in English.

## 1. Planning Overview

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| Purpose | Ride the autumn (Oct–Nov) camping peak with seasonal, save-worthy tips |
| Core message | Prepare for the big day/night temperature gap, eat simple and warm, enjoy the campfire — and stay safe from carbon monoxide |
| Tone | Warm, friendly, light; short sentences, emoji in moderation |
| Goal metrics | Saves and shares (practical checklists), comments ("where are you going this weekend?") |

### Background (from research)

- Autumn brings cool weather, fewer bugs and fall foliage, but also a **large day/night temperature gap**, which pushes campers to use heaters inside tents.
- Fire services counted **153 camping gas-poisoning accidents in 2021–2023**; in 2023, **11 of 15 cardiac arrests during camping** were caused by gas poisoning.
- Carbon monoxide has no color or smell. Key prevention: CO alarm, ventilation, turning heaters off before sleep, and never burning charcoal inside a tent or car.
- Trend: "slow down and simplify" (KOA 2026) fits a cozy, minimal autumn-camping mood.
